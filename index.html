Employee Sales Dashboard Project (Excel)

I developed a dynamic Employee Sales Performance Dashboard using Excel to analyze over 500 sales records across multiple regions and departments. I cleaned the dataset using SUBSTITUTE, CLEAN, VALUE, MID, LEFT, and RIGHT functions, then used IF statements to categorize employees by performance level. Through conditional formatting, I highlighted top performers and applied a color scale for quick visual insight. I also implemented data validation with a drop-down list that allows users to select an employee’s name and instantly view their department, region, and total sales. Finally, I built interactive PivotTables and charts to display key metrics such as total sales, top 10 employees, and quarterly performance by region — resulting in a clear, interactive, and professional dashboard that tells a data-driven story.

Full Summary of Project

This project demonstrates my ability to clean, analyze, and visualize large datasets using Microsoft Excel. The dataset contained over 500 employee sales records across multiple regions and departments. My goal was to evaluate employee sales performance and generate insights through dynamic dashboards. Data Cleaning & Preparation Applied SUBSTITUTE, CLEAN, and VALUE functions to remove unwanted symbols, spaces, and text formatting issues. Used LEFT, RIGHT, and MID functions to extract and correct text and numerical values from mixed data cells. Standardized sales figures and ensured consistent number formatting for analysis. Verified data accuracy by checking for duplicates and inconsistencies. Data Analysis Calculated Total Sales per Employee by summing quarterly figures (Q1–Q4). Created an IF statement to categorize employees into performance groups: High Performer – ≥ 200K Mid Performer – 180K–199K Low Performer – < 180K Added a Performance Rating and Ranking System to identify top contributors. Conditional Formatting Highlighted the Top 10 Highest Producers in green for quick recognition. Applied three-color scales (green–yellow–red) to visualize performance ratings (best, mid, and low). Used color-coded sales tables to enhance readability and insight. Data Visualization (Dashboard) Designed an interactive Excel Dashboard with PivotTables and PivotCharts to summarize key insights: Total Sales Overview and Quarterly Sales Trends Top 10 Employees by Total Sales Sales by Department and Sales by Region KPI Cards displaying total sales, department count, and regional performance Implemented slicers and filters for real-time interaction and dynamic exploration of data. Tools & Techniques Microsoft Excel (Formulas, Conditional Formatting, PivotTables, and Dashboards) Data Cleaning Functions: SUBSTITUTE, VALUE, CLEAN, MID, LEFT, RIGHT Logical and Analytical Functions: IF, SUM, RANK Visualization: Charts, Color Scales, and KPI Cards Key Outcome The final dashboard provided a clear, interactive view of employee performance, enabling quick identification of high-performing regions, departments, and individuals. It also showcased my ability to manage end-to-end data projects — from raw data cleaning to professional visualization.
